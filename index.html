Live on your fleet in three steps.

Most teams see their first forecast inside a day. Full rollout averages under two weeks

  1. 01

    Connect your fleet

    Drop the agent on one host per site, or point BaseLine at your existing collectors. Every node, rack and VLAN is discovered on its own.

  2. 02

    Set baselines and rules

    BaseLine learns each node's normal in a few days. You decide what counts as critical and who owns it, per site or per service

  3. 03

    Route to on-call

    Critical events page the right owner with full context, open the ticket and post to the channel. Nobody forwards an alert again.
